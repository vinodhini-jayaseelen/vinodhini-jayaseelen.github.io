# vinodhini-jayaseelen.github.io
Stochastic Analysis, rough volatility & quantum computing for derivative pricing

My current independent research focuses on **Quantum Monte Carlo and Quantum Amplitude Estimation for derivative pricing**, building on my previous work in rough volatility, fractional Kolmogorov equations, Monte Carlo simulation and scientific Machine Learning (Sci ML).  

---

## Quantum Computing & Derivative Pricing

I am exploring how quantum algorithms can be applied to expectation estimation problems arising in derivative pricing, with particular interest in: 

- Quantum Amplitude Estimation
- Quantum Monte Carlo Estimation
- Classical vs quantum MC
- Quantum methods for derivative pricing
- Stochastic-volatility models

My initial work focuses on simple derivative-pricing problems before moving toward stochastic-volatility and rough-volatility models.

## Background: Rough Volatility & Scientific Computing 

### Physics-Informed Neural Operators (PINO) for Fractional Kolmogorov Equations 

During my MRes research, I investigated computational alternatives to Monte Carlo for fractional Kolmogorov equations in super-rough volatility, using PINO as the main framework. This work motivated me to continue exploring quantum approaches to Monte Carlo-based expectation estimation.
